Privacy Policy

Party Games! New Year Fun
Effective date: November 3, 2026 · Last updated: November 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Party Games! New Year Fun (“the App”, “we”, “us” or “our”) handles information when you use the App. Please read it carefully.

1. Information you enter and information stored on your device

The App lets players enter names and use those names during party games. This information is used to operate the games, such as identifying whose turn it is and displaying scores. Game information may be stored locally on your device so that the App can function.

We do not require you to create an account, provide your name, or submit player names to us. Do not enter sensitive or confidential information as a player name. Anyone with access to your device may be able to see information saved in the App.

2. Advertising and third-party services

The App may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob. AdMob and its partners may collect or receive information from your device, such as advertising identifiers, IP address, device and app information, approximate location derived from IP address, ad interactions, and diagnostic data. The information processed and the purposes of processing can depend on your device settings, consent choices, and the ad services available in your region.

Google may use this information to provide, measure, limit, and improve advertising, including personalized or non-personalized ads where permitted. We use Google’s consent tools, where required, to present applicable privacy and advertising choices. Your choices may affect which advertisements are shown.

For more information, please review Google’s Privacy Policy and Google’s information about how AdMob uses data.

3. How we use information

Information handled by the App and its advertising services may be used to:

  • Run the games, manage turns, and calculate scores.
  • Remember relevant game settings or locally stored game information.
  • Display and measure advertisements, where enabled.
  • Maintain, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the App and its services.
  • Meet legal, regulatory, and platform requirements.

4. Sharing of information

We do not sell the player names or game information you enter into the App. Information may be processed by Google and its advertising partners when advertisements are provided. We may also disclose information if required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect the rights, safety, and security of users or the App.

5. Data retention and deletion

Player names, scores, preferences, and other game data stored locally remain on your device until they are deleted within the App (if that option is available), the App’s data is cleared, or the App is uninstalled. The retention of information handled by Google AdMob is governed by Google’s policies and settings.

6. Your choices and privacy rights

You can stop using the App and remove locally stored App data by clearing the App’s storage or uninstalling it. Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access to, correction or deletion of personal information, to object to or restrict certain processing, or to withdraw consent. Where information is processed by Google for advertising, you may also need to exercise applicable choices through Google or your device settings.

For privacy requests relating to information under our control, contact us using the details in the Contact section below. We may need to verify your request before responding.

7. Children’s privacy

The App offers family-friendly party-game content, but it is not intended to encourage children to provide personal information. The App does not ask users to create accounts or submit personal details to us. Player names entered for gameplay are stored locally on the device as described above. Advertising services may process device information as described in this policy and may apply age-related or regional safeguards where configured or required.

Parents or guardians should supervise children’s use of the App and should avoid allowing children to enter real full names or other personal information. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us directly, please contact us so we can review the matter.

8. Security

We take reasonable steps appropriate to the App to protect information under our control. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. Because game information may be stored on your device, protect your device with appropriate access controls.

9. International users

You may use the App in different countries. Advertising and other third-party services may process information in countries other than the country where you live, subject to the service providers’ terms, safeguards, and applicable law.

10. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App, our practices, or applicable legal requirements change. We will update the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after an updated policy is made available means the updated policy applies to subsequent use.
